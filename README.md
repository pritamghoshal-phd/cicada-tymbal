# The tymbal of a cicada: nature's sound-generating metastructure

Project page for our paper in *Nature Communications* 16, 10270 (2025).

**Live page:** https://pritamghoshal-phd.github.io/cicada-tymbal/
**Paper:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-65149-5
**Code:** https://doi.org/10.24433/CO.7145974.v1
**Species data:** https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30025519

Cicadas make sound when the ribs of the tymbal membrane snap through one after another, and the body filters that
broadband clicking into a species-specific song. We model the tymbal as a chain of bistable von Mises trusses
coupled to a chain of linear resonators, and show that one tunable model reproduces the songs of several species.
